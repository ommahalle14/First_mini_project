Feeding Animals: Do it Responsibly

Feeding animals, especially strays or birds, can be kind, but must be done with care. Wrong foods can harm them, and overfeeding can make them dependent.

  • Use proper food for each species (no bread for birds).
  • Feed only in clean places, away from roads.
  • Never leave plastic or wrappers.
  • Provide water during hot days.
Responsible feeding promotes trust, safety, and health for both humans and animals.

The Controversy of Sea Reclamation

Sea reclamation — the act of creating new land from oceans or coastal regions — has become increasingly common in urban development. However, these projects come with serious ecological consequences that cannot be ignored.

Environmental Impact:
  • Destroys marine biodiversity including fish, corals, and mangroves.
  • Displaces coastal communities and traditional fishermen.
  • Increases the risk of flooding and erosion in the long term.
  • Pollutes water bodies with debris and construction waste.

It’s alarming how such projects often proceed despite public protests and environmental warnings. Governments justify these actions in the name of development or tourism, but the irreversible harm to ecosystems is a steep price to pay.

How can we allow destruction of our seas in the name of progress? Environmental clearances are sometimes given hastily, without proper impact assessments or public consent. Nature is not a commodity — it's a legacy we must protect.
